# Domain Glossary: AI Japanese Teacher

## Core Concepts

### Daily Lesson
A single study session consisting of a thematic **Lesson Topic**, a curated **Target Vocabulary** set, a contextual **Generated Passage**, and synchronized **Audio**.

### Target Vocabulary
A curated set of Japanese words/kanji (typically 3–5 items) selected for a session. Words are chosen to be semantically and topically cohesive (e.g., "ordering at a café" or "train travel"), graded to the learner's chosen proficiency level (JLPT N5 through N1).

### Generated Passage
A natural, level-appropriate Japanese text (dialogue, short story, or situational scenario) generated by the AI language model. It embeds all **Target Vocabulary** in natural context, with Furigana (ruby text) support and sentence-by-sentence segmentation.

### Furigana Annotation
Phonetic reading aids (Hiragana) rendered directly above Kanji characters using HTML `<ruby>` / `<rt>` markup or tokenized JSON structures.

### Audio Provider
An interchangeable speech synthesis adapter responsible for rendering spoken Japanese from text. Supports speed modification (e.g. 0.75x–1.25x), sentence looping, and pluggable backends (Native Device Speech API, Google Cloud Neural TTS, ElevenLabs).
