# Domain Glossary: AI Japanese Teacher

## Core Concepts

### Daily Lesson
A single study session consisting of a thematic **Lesson Topic**, a curated **Target Vocabulary** set (3–5 items), a contextual **Generated Passage** (dialogue or story), and synchronized **Audio**.

### Target Vocabulary
A curated set of Japanese words/kanji selected for a session. Words are chosen to be semantically and topically cohesive (e.g., "ordering at a café" or "train travel"), graded to the learner's chosen proficiency level (JLPT N5 through N1). Each word includes Kanji, Furigana, Romaji, English definition, part of speech, and 3 audio-supported context examples.

### Two-Phase Lesson Generation
An asynchronous lesson creation lifecycle split into two stages:
1. **Phase 1 (Target Vocabulary Primer)**: Rapid generation of target words and context examples, immediately transitioning the learner to study mode and persisting words to the Word Bank.
2. **Phase 2 (Dialogue Passage Background Generation)**: Concurrent generation of a conversational roleplay passage embedding the Phase 1 vocabulary.

### Learned Word Exclusion Context
Negative context supplied to the lesson generation prompt containing existing learned words from the Word Bank to prevent duplicate vocabulary across daily lessons.

### Generated Passage & Speaker Differentiation
A natural, level-appropriate Japanese dialogue generated by the AI language model embedding all Target Vocabulary in context. Dialogue lines are rendered with distinct speaker tints, badge roles (Speaker A, Speaker B, Narrator), and Furigana tokenization.

### Furigana Annotation
Phonetic reading aids (Hiragana) rendered directly above Kanji characters using tokenized structures (`surface`, `reading`, `isTarget`).

### Audio Provider & Paced Playback
An interchangeable speech synthesis adapter responsible for rendering spoken Japanese from text. Supports speed modification (0.75x–1.25x), sentence looping, configurable inter-sentence pauses (`PASSAGE_SENTENCE_GAP_MS = 800ms`), and pluggable backends (Native Device Speech API via `expo-speech`, Google Cloud Neural TTS, ElevenLabs).

### Learned Word Bank
A persistent, cumulative dictionary of all vocabulary words encountered across daily lessons. Features a compact single-line list format with on-demand accordion expansion for context examples and detailed readings.

### Lesson Retention Policy (FIFO + Starred Pinning)
Auto-saved un-starred lesson dialogues are stored in local storage with a FIFO capacity limit (e.g., 25 recent lessons) to prevent storage bloat. Lessons marked as **⭐ Starred / Favorite** bypass the FIFO eviction queue and are stored permanently.

### Word List Import (Batched LLM Enrichment)
A feature in Settings allowing users to paste a raw plain-text list of Japanese words/kanji (newline, comma, or space-separated). The text is processed in asynchronous batches (6–8 words each) via the language model to extract readings, meanings, parts of speech, and 3 example sentences, inserting enriched entries directly into the cumulative Word Bank with duplicate prevention.

### Practice Passage (Word Bank Immersion Mode)
An on-demand reading and listening study mode accessible from the Learn screen that generates authentic Japanese dialogue or story passages utilizing words from the user's existing Word Bank. Operates in two modes:
1. **Auto Practice**: Automatically selects 6–10 least-recently practiced words to guarantee even spaced-repetition coverage across the entire dictionary over time.
2. **Custom Selection**: Allows manual selection of specific Word Bank words for focused passage generation.
Furigana is set to **hidden (off) by default** to encourage active reading recognition.

### Review Vocabulary Recycling
The practice of supplying a subset of previously learned Word Bank entries as secondary context during Daily Lesson (Phase 2) dialogue generation, prompting the model to naturally weave review words alongside fresh target vocabulary to boost long-term retention.

### Core Navigation Surfaces
- 🏠 **Learn**: Dual Mode Hub offering **Daily Graded Lesson** (new vocabulary + dialogue) and **Word Bank Practice Passage** (immersion with existing vocabulary), topic suggestions, and active 2-screen lesson study.
- 📖 **History**: Chronological log of past lessons with search, JLPT filters, and Starred filter.
- 🗂️ **Word Bank**: Cumulative dictionary of all learned and imported words with compact list display, multi-accordion expansion, search, and individual audio playback.
- ⚙️ **Settings**: Gemini API key management, Word List Import tool, Japanese voice selection, default speed preference, default JLPT level, and local data management.

### Vocabulary Constraint Tiers
A user-configurable constraint level governing how strictly passage generation (both Daily Lesson dialogues and Practice Passages) is bounded by the user's learned words:
1. **Strict Closed Bank**: 100% of content words (nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs) must originate strictly from the user's Word Bank (plus the current session's Target Vocabulary in Daily Lessons), bound together with level-appropriate Japanese grammatical particles and standard copulas/inflections. Zero unlearned content words.
2. **Comprehensible Input (i+1)**: 85–90% Word Bank vocabulary, intentionally introducing 1–2 level-appropriate novel words in context with explicit glossing.
3. **Natural Graded Immersion**: Free-form level-appropriate Japanese dialogue embedding the target words without strict Word Bank confinement.

### Known Vocabulary Inventory
The complete collection of user-acquired Word Bank entries (surfaces, readings, and core definitions) injected directly into the language model's generation prompt to serve as an authoritative closed-dictionary boundary for Strict and i+1 passage generation.

### Gemini Model Configuration
The application is configured with the following active Gemini model identifiers:
- Primary default: `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`
- Fallback models cascade: `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`, `gemini-3.6-flash`, `gemini-3.7-flash`, `gemini-3.5-flash`

> [!NOTE]
> These model identifiers have been validated as active and working in this environment. Future revisions should leave these model names intact and not revert them.

## Architecture Roadmap & Optimization Notes

### Client-Side Japanese Tokenization (Kuromoji / MeCab)
- **Goal**: Offload Furigana parsing and token segmentation from Gemini to a client-side library (e.g. `kuromoji.js` or a lightweight dictionary tokenizer).
- **Benefit**: Reduces LLM response payload by ~60% (generating only raw Japanese text + English translations rather than large structured token arrays), significantly speeding up passage generation times and decreasing token costs.
